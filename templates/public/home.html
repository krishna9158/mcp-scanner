8. Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers without legal confusion.

Do I need to understand the DPDP Act before signing up?

No. TrustAudit guides you through plain-English questions about your business, suggests what applies to you, and shows only your next three practical steps.

What happens after I click Check My Business?

You create a workspace account in under a minute, walk through five simple setup steps (you can save and return anytime), and land on your personalised action list.

Does TrustAudit mark my business as certified automatically?

No. Suggestions remain drafts until you confirm them, and TrustAudit is operational readiness software—not a government certification body or law firm. Detailed statutory schedules (including 13 November 2025, 13 November 2026, S.O. 5458(E), and 13 May 2027) stay available in our Resources Library.

How does Website Check inspect my website safely?

It checks only publicly visible web pages (Privacy Notice links, public enquiry forms, headers, and external scripts) and never reads private databases or internal accounts.
