16 • Frequently Asked Questions

Straight Answers Before You Sign Up

Does TrustAudit grant government certification or replace legal counsel? ›

No. Neither MeitY nor the Data Protection Board certifies SaaS compliance tools. TrustAudit organises your customer data map, action tasks, notices, vendor DPAs, and verifiable evidence packs for management and legal counsel review.

Is TrustAudit a registered Consent Manager under Section 6(9) & Rule 4? ›

No. TrustAudit provides internal Data Fiduciary workflows for recording consents, withdrawal requests, and privacy notices—it does not hold Consent Manager registration.

What does Public Website Check inspect vs internal systems? ›

Website Check inspects publicly reachable URLs safely under SSRF controls (HTTPS, public forms, consent checkboxes, third-party script domains, and grievance links). It never guesses internal databases or private records.

Are ₹50 crore to ₹250 crore Schedule ceilings automatic penalties? ›

No. These figures are statutory maximum ceilings in the Schedule to the DPDP Act, 2023. Penalties are determined case by case by the Data Protection Board considering Section 33 factors.
