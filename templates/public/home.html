15 • Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers Before You Start

Does TrustAudit certify legal compliance?

No. Neither MeitY nor the Data Protection Board certifies SaaS compliance tools. TrustAudit organises your inventory, assessments, action tasks, and evidence files cleanly so your team and legal counsel stay prepared.

Do I have to type every customer data record manually?

No. Onboarding proposes industry-specific activities, inspects public website forms/scripts, and parses uploaded CSV, XLSX, DOCX, or PDF documents into draft suggestions for you to confirm, edit, or reject.

Is TrustAudit a registered Consent Manager under Section 6(9)?

No. TrustAudit helps Data Fiduciaries organise consent records and notices internally and is not a Section 6(9) Consent Manager.

Are the ₹50 crore to ₹250 crore DPDP penalties automatic?

No. These are statutory maximum ceilings in the Schedule to the DPDP Act, 2023. Penalties are determined case by case by the Data Protection Board under Section 33 factors.
